Scoring framework

Six criteria, weights you control

Each company is rated 1–5 on every criterion. The weighted score out of 100 decides the call: 75+ advance, 60–74 watch, below 60 pass.

Returns model

What the cheque could return

Entry ownership is diluted by each later round, then multiplied by the exit value. Three scenarios, weighted by your probabilities, give the expected multiple.

Methodology

How the system runs each quarter

  1. Map the universe

    Pull every African fintech that raised in the last 24 months, plus scout and accelerator lists.

  2. Screen

    Filter on stage (Seed+ to Series A), ticket fit ($2–8M) and inclusion thesis.

  3. Score

    Two partners rate the six criteria independently. Gaps over one point go to discussion.

  4. Syndicate

    Match each IC-ready deal to a lead and two co-investors from the shortlist.

  5. Refresh

    Re-score, re-stage and re-run returns every quarter, or on any new round.
