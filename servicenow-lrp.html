ServiceNow five-year plan: can AI growth outrun seat pressure?

A driver-based long-range plan for ServiceNow (NYSE: NOW), built from its public filings. It forecasts revenue, margins, opex, headcount, cash flow and SaaS metrics for FY2026–FY2030 under three scenarios, tests which assumptions matter most, and evaluates an investment proposal against the plan.

What the plan says

Conclusions first; the model is how I got there.

  1. Growth is the decision that matters.Two points of subscription growth a year, up or down, moves FY2030 free cash flow by roughly ±$950M, more than twice any single cost lever tested. The central planning question is whether AI products grow faster than AI shrinks seat-based licences.
  2. Margin expansion comes from operating leverage, led by sales & marketing.Base-case non-GAAP operating margin rises ~270 bp from FY2026’s guided 31.5% to 34.2% by FY2030. Opex falls 4.6 points as a share of revenue, 2.9 of them in sales & marketing.
  3. FCF margin stays flat even as operating margin rises.Working-capital inflows from upfront billing grow more slowly than revenue as growth decelerates, and cash taxes rise. A plan that promises both slowing growth and rising FCF margin needs a reason.
  4. AI productivity reshapes the headcount plan.The upside case reaches ~$2.1B more revenue than base with slightly fewer employees, because revenue per employee compounds faster.

Evaluating an investment proposal

Illustrative case: fund an AI go-to-market team in FY2027–28 to accelerate AI product attach. Inputs are assumptions, not ServiceNow data.

Recommendation: stage-gate

NPV is positive, but almost all of it sits in the terminal value and payback misses a four-year hurdle. Fund the first tranche; release the second only if AI attach rates hit target.

How the model works

Every forecast line is a reported figure or one driver applied to another line. No hidden plugs.

Revenue & SaaS metrics

Subscription revenue grows at a scenario growth rate; cRPO is tied to subscription revenue; Rule of 40 = growth + FCF margin.

Margins & opex

GAAP gross margin and S&M, R&D, G&A as % of revenue; non-GAAP adds back stock comp and acquisition amortization.

Cash & headcount

Cash flow builds from non-GAAP operating income, cash taxes, deferred-revenue inflows and capex; headcount = revenue ÷ revenue per employee.

Free cash flow = non-GAAP operating income − cash taxes
               + working-capital inflow + net interest − capex

The plan exists in Python and as a formula-driven Excel workbook; an automated check confirms the two agree in every scenario, and 13 tests confirm historicals tie to filings. I used Claude as a coding and drafting partner and verified every number against ServiceNow’s filings.
